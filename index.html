Read it like an address

Each word zooms into the one before it: the year, the Sun's half of that year, a season inside that half, a month inside the season, a fortnight inside the month, and the lunar day inside the fortnight. The weekday and the Moon's star are two separate cycles that also name the day.

    Tap any word to see why it is today's word.

    How the masa gets its name

    • Sun's path during this lunar month (new moon to new moon)
    • Sankranti: the Sun enters a new rashi
    • No sankranti inside: adhika (extra) month

    About these calculations

    Positions come from Astronomy Engine. Tithi, nakshatra and weekday are taken at sunrise in the city you pick, so they can differ between Hyderabad and New Jersey on the same date.

    This is an early prototype. Things to know:

    • The ayanamsha is a linear approximation of Lahiri, not the official value. Near a boundary, a published panchangam can differ.
    • Ritu is counted from the lunar masa and ayana from the sidereal Sun. Some families count these differently.
    • Festival dates follow extra rules (for example, which part of the day a tithi must cover). This page shows the calendar, not festival decisions.
    • The words are shown in their dictionary form. The exact recited form of your sankalpam depends on your tradition; follow your teacher.
